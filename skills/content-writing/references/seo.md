# SEO Guidance (Shared by Technical and Marketing)

Help the target reader find the article without damaging readability through keyword stuffing.

## Keywords

- Choose one primary keyword and 2–3 related long-tail terms. Use the primary term naturally in the title, opening, a subheading, and body; avoid excessive density.
- Before selecting a topic, compare high-ranking results for the target terms and determine how this article will differ.
- Never sacrifice natural language for keyword placement. Write for people first, then make necessary alignment for search engines.

## Title and Summary

- Include the primary keyword and make the reader's takeaway clear. Avoid clickbait and interchangeable titles.
- A useful pattern is a concrete subject and visible benefit, for example: `<subject>: <how to do it> (<scope/limitation>)`.
- Aim for a 120–160-character meta description that states the thesis and one reason to click.

## Structure

- Use clear heading levels and natural keyword variants. Use lists, code blocks, and quotations where they improve readability and eligibility for rich results.
- The opening should explain within 30 seconds who the article is for, what it solves, and whether it is worth reading. This helps both SEO and retention.

## Check

- Confirm the primary keyword appears naturally in the title, opening, at least one subheading, and body.
- Find sentences made less readable merely to include a keyword; rewrite them naturally.
